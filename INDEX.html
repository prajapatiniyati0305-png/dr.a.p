About Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Dr. Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was one of India's most respected scientists, educators and public figures. He was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. After completing his studies in physics and aerospace engineering, he joined India's space and defence research organisations.

Dr. Kalam played an important role in the development of India's space and missile programmes and became popularly known as the "Missile Man of India". He worked with organisations such as ISRO and DRDO and contributed to important national technology programmes.

He served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007. He was admired for his simple lifestyle, positive attitude and dedication to students. Even after his presidency, he continued visiting educational institutions and interacting with students.
